# Lead Meeting Prep — UAT P1 (S-LMP-3)

> **Mục tiêu:** 2 lead thật — AM dùng Sales Cockpit (talk track + offer ladder 3 gói).  
> **Gate:** `bash scripts/lmp_p1_gate.sh`

## Checklist AM (mỗi lead)

1. Mở lead B2B → tab **Sales Cockpit**
2. Chờ prep **Sẵn sàng** (4 bước: Thu thập → Xác minh → Chiến lược → Vũ trang)
3. **Intel:** đọc pain/ROI, urgency, competitive
4. **Talk Track:** ≥3 phase SPIN, copy script, timer
5. **Offer Ladder:** 3 cột CB / **TC** / CS — TC highlighted
6. **Objections:** mở flip card, đọc rebuttal
7. 👍/👎 feedback — kiểm tra DB `crm_lead_meeting_prep_feedback`
